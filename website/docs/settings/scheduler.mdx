---
title: Scheduler
description: Rules that change the speed limits and connection limits at the times of the week you choose, and stop or resume the downloads of a category.
---

The **Scheduler** changes njMule's limits at the times you choose: slower uploads during office
hours, no download limit at night, fewer connections at the weekend. Each rule has days of the
week, a window of time and the limits it sets; when it begins, it can also stop the downloads of
one category and resume those of another. The web interface has the same scheduler, under
**Options → Scheduler**.

## The rules

The list shows each rule with its **Name**, **Days**, **From** and **To** times, **Upload limit**,
**Download limit** and whether it is **On**. Beside the buttons under the form, njMule says which
rule applies now, as *Now: Office hours*, or *No rule applies now*. With no rules, the list says
*No rules: the preferences always apply.*

A rule applies as soon as you add, replace or remove it; there is nothing else to save.

### Add a rule

1. Fill in the form under the list: name, days, times and the values the rule sets.
2. Click **Add**. The rule goes to the end of the list.

### Change or remove a rule

1. Click the rule in the list: the form shows its values.
2. Change them and click **Replace selected**, or click **Remove** to delete the rule.

**Add** and **Replace selected** stay off while a field of the form holds a wrong value.

## The form of a rule

| Field | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | empty | A name of your choice, shown in the list and in *Now:*. Up to 100 characters. |
| **On** | ticked | Only a rule that is on applies; untick it to keep a rule without using it. |
| **Days** | none | The days of the week the rule starts on. A rule with no day ticked never applies. |
| **From / To** | `00:00` / `00:00` | The window of the rule, as `hh:mm` in 24-hour time. When **To** is earlier than **From**, the window runs past midnight into the next day. When they are equal, the rule holds the whole day. |
| **Upload limit** | empty | The upload limit during the window, in **KB/s** or **MB/s**, or **No limit**. Empty leaves the limit of the options. |
| **Download limit** | empty | The download limit during the window, in the same way. |
| **Max. connections** | empty | The most connections open at once during the window: a whole number from 1 to 10,000. Empty leaves the option's value. |
| **Max. Sources/File** | empty | The most sources of one download during the window: a whole number from 1 to 10,000. Empty leaves the option's value. |
| **Cat:Stop Downloads** | **-** | When the rule begins, stops the downloads of this category. **-** stops none; **Uncategorized** means the downloads in no category. |
| **Cat:Resume Downloads** | **-** | When the rule begins, resumes the paused and stopped downloads of this category. |

## How rules apply

- **The first rule that holds wins.** njMule goes down the list in order and takes the first rule
  that is on and whose window holds now; put the most specific rules first.
- **A rule replaces the options, it does not change them.** While it holds, its values take the
  place of the limits of [Bandwidth](bandwidth.mdx) and [Connection](connection.mdx); a field left
  empty keeps the option's value. The options stay as you set them and apply again as soon as no
  rule holds. A limit you set meanwhile from the top bar changes the options, which the rule still
  overrides until it ends.
- **The times are your computer's local time.**
- **A window past midnight belongs to the day it starts on.** A rule from `22:00` to `06:00` with
  only Friday ticked holds from Friday 22:00 to Saturday 06:00.
- **Categories are acted on once, when the rule begins.** Nothing is stopped or resumed again while
  it holds, nor undone when it ends. Editing a rule while it holds does not repeat its actions.

The log says when a rule begins (*Scheduler: "Night" applies now*), which categories it stopped or
resumed, and when the options apply again (*Scheduler: the preferences apply again*).

## Where the rules are kept

The rules are kept in `scheduler.properties` in the configuration folder, one group of `rule.N.*`
keys per rule, in the order of the list. A rule that cannot be read is left out, and the log says
so. A field left empty has no key. For example:

```properties
# njMule scheduler
rule.1.name=Night
rule.1.enabled=true
rule.1.days=MONDAY,TUESDAY,WEDNESDAY,THURSDAY,FRIDAY
rule.1.start=01:00
rule.1.end=07:00
rule.1.downloadKiBps=0
rule.2.name=Office hours
rule.2.enabled=true
rule.2.days=MONDAY,TUESDAY,WEDNESDAY,THURSDAY,FRIDAY
rule.2.start=09:00
rule.2.end=18:00
rule.2.uploadKiBps=30
rule.2.downloadKiBps=200
rule.2.stopCategory=2
```

The limits are in KiB/s, `0` for no limit. The categories are their numbers, `0` for the downloads
in none (see [Categories](../desktop/categories.mdx)).

## In the web interface

**Options → Scheduler** in the web interface shows the rule that applies now, the same list and the
same form. There a new rule starts with every day ticked, the times are chosen in steps of 15
minutes, and the limits are typed in KB/s, `0` for no limit.
