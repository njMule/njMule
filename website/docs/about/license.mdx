### What njMule owes to eMule

njMule is an independent re-implementation of the eMule file-sharing client in Java. Its protocol
constants, file formats and algorithms were translated from eMule's source code, the lower-case
table Kad applies to keywords is taken from eMule's resource files, and the interface texts and
their translations are converted from eMule's resource files. eMule is copyright (C) 2002-2026
Merkur and the eMule contributors ([emule-project.net](https://www.emule-project.net)), licensed
under the GNU GPL version 2 or any later version; the material derived from it is used in njMule
under version 3. The community version of eMule at
[github.com/irwir/eMule](https://github.com/irwir/eMule) served as the reference.

### Fonts, flags and data

- **Manrope**, Bold and ExtraBold: the face of the desktop app's titles and of its wordmark,
  bundled with it. SIL Open Font License 1.1, from
  [github.com/googlefonts/manrope](https://github.com/googlefonts/manrope).
- **flag-icons**: the country flags shown beside clients, rendered to small PNG pictures. MIT
  License, from [github.com/lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons).
- **DB-IP IP to Country Lite**: not shipped. When you choose to see the countries of other clients
  (off by default), njMule downloads this database once a month into its cache. It is licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
  [IP Geolocation by DB-IP](https://db-ip.com).
- **The server list and Kad contacts** a new profile starts from: the `server.met` and `nodes.dat`
  published at [upd.emule-security.org](https://upd.emule-security.org), downloaded unchanged on
  15 October 2026. They hold public addresses, ports and server names, facts not covered by
  copyright (recorded as `CC0-1.0` in `REUSE.toml`).

### Libraries linked statically

The fully static Linux x64 executable, inside the Synology DSM package, the
`njmule-<version>-linux-x64-static.tar.xz` archive and the `linux/amd64` Docker image, contains
**musl libc** (MIT License) and **zlib** (zlib License). Their licence texts ship with it.

## The complete list

- **`NOTICE`**, the file of copyright, attribution and third-party notices, ships with the Windows
  installer and zip (as `NOTICE.txt` or `NOTICE`, beside the programs), the Linux packages (in
  `/usr/share/doc/njmule`), the Linux archives, the Synology package and the Docker image (in
  `/usr/share/doc/njmule`). Every njMule prints it with `njmule --notice`. Read it online in
  [`NOTICE`](https://github.com/njMule/njMule/blob/main/NOTICE).
- **The software bill of materials** of each release, `njmule-<version>-sbom.cdx.json` (CycloneDX),
  lists every component with its version.
- **The web interface's browser app** lists its packages and their licence texts at
  `/THIRD-PARTY.md` of the web interface, such as `http://localhost:4711/THIRD-PARTY.md`.
- **From the sources**, `./gradlew :njmule-gui:generateLicenseReport` writes `THIRD-PARTY.txt`, the
  full list with versions, and `./gradlew cyclonedxBom` the bill of materials. See
  [Dependencies](/developer/dependencies).
