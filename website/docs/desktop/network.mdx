**Network** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>5</kbd>) tells how njMule stands on the two networks and holds
everything about them. At the top, three cards say in plain words whether others reach you on eD2K
and on Kad, and which ports you use. Below them a switch shows one of three parts, each with a count:
**Servers** (how many servers you know), **Kad** (how many contacts, while Kad runs) and **Log**.
The summary under the title gives the state of both networks, as `eD2K: Lighthouse (HighID) · Kad:
Open`. The sidebar's dot on **Network** tells the same at a glance.

To learn what eD2K, Kad, HighID and LowID are, see [Servers and Kad](../network/servers-and-kad.mdx).

## The cards

| Card | What it says | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| **eD2K** | **Reachable from outside** (a HighID) or **Not reachable from outside** (a LowID), **Connecting** or **Not Connected**; the server you are on, with its users and files. The tooltip of the state gives the technical term. | **Connect**, **Cancel** or **Disconnect**; **Choose another server…** opens the server list. |
| **Kad** | **Reachable from outside** (open) or **Not reachable from outside** (firewalled), **Connecting** or **Off**; the number of contacts, and whether UDP is open or firewalled once tested. | **Connect** or **Disconnect**; **Recheck Firewall** asks other nodes again whether they reach you. |
| **Ports** | Your ports, as `TCP 4662 · UDP 4672` (the UDP port while Kad runs, which listens on it); what the router answered through UPnP, such as **The router at 192.168.1.1 forwards the ports** or **UPnP is off**; your public IPv6 address when you have one, and whether **NAT traversal** works (it needs Kad). | **Test Ports**; **Options** opens the options. |

The ports and UPnP are set in the [Connection options](../settings/connection.mdx); NAT traversal is
explained on [NAT traversal](../network/nat-traversal.mdx) and IPv6 on [IPv6](../network/ipv6.mdx).

### Port test

**Test Ports** checks whether others can reach your ports, and shows what it finds as it comes:

1. When UPnP is on, njMule asks the router again to forward the ports, and says whether it did:
   **The router at … forwards the ports**, **forwards only some of the ports**, **refused to forward
   the ports**, **No router answered through UPnP**, or **UPnP is off**.
2. The server checks your TCP port at a new login (njMule connects to the server again), and Kad
   nodes check both ports.
3. Each port then reads **others reach it**, or **others cannot reach it; forward it in the router or
   allow it in the firewall**, or **not tested, as no server or Kad node could do it**. Without Kad,
   UDP is **not tested, Kad is off**; Kad's UDP test may take a few minutes.

The same test is in the first-start wizard and the Connection options. If a port stays closed, see
[Ports](../network/ports.mdx).

## Servers

The **Servers** part lists the eD2K servers you know. **Servers (…)** above the list counts them;
**Filter servers** keeps those whose name, address, description or version hold every word you type
(<kbd>Esc</kbd> empties it).

The server you are connected to is in bold, with an accent bar and the tooltip **Connected to this
server**; the one being tried is in italics (**Connecting to this server**).

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Server Name**, **IP**, **Description** | What the server calls itself, its address and port, and its description. |
| **Ping** | How long it took to answer, in milliseconds. |
| **Users**, **Max Users**, **Files** | How many use it, how many it takes, how many files it knows. |
| **Priority** | **Low**, **Normal** or **High**: higher ones are tried first. |
| **Failed** | Failed attempts in a row. Dead servers leave the list after a number of them, as the [Server options](../settings/server.mdx) say; static servers and the one you are on stay. |
| **Static** | Whether it is in your static list. |
| **Soft File Limit**, **Hard File Limit** | How many of your files it takes (the hard limit is hidden at first). |
| **Version** | Its software version (hidden at first). |
| **Low ID** | How many of its users have a LowID. |
| **Obfuscation** | Whether it takes obfuscated connections. |

The columns keep their widths and the list scrolls sideways.

### The details of a server

Choose a server to see its details beside the list (over its end, with a cross, in a narrow window):
its name, address and description; **Connect** or **Disconnect**; **Add To Static List** or **Remove
From Static List**; **⋯** with **Priority**, **Copy eD2K Links** and **Remove**; its figures (users,
files, limits, Low ID users, ping, version, failures, priority); and, for the server you are on,
the last messages it sent under **Server Info**.

### Connecting to a server

Connect to the server chosen with **Connect** in its details, **Connect To** in its menu, a double
click, or <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Connecting to another server leaves the one you are on. To let njMule
choose, use **Connect** in the top bar's [network menu](./interface.mdx#the-network-menu).

### Adding servers

**Add servers** opens the ways to add them; an empty list offers them too:

<Screenshot
    id="servers-add"
    alt="The Add servers popup: Update server.met from URL with an address and an Update button, IP or Address with Connect to address, and Add servers from server.met"
    caption="The ways to add servers"
/>

- **Update server.met from URL**: type or choose the address of a `server.met` list and press
  **Update**. The box offers the addresses used before; while you used none, it offers a well-known
  list. njMule fetches nothing until you press **Update**, only over http or https, and says how many
  new servers it added.
- **IP or Address**: type a server's address as `host:port` and press **Connect to address**: the
  server joins the list and njMule connects to it.
- **Add servers from server.met...**: adds the servers of a `server.met` file on your computer.

Server links work too: `ed2k://|server|…|/` and `ed2k://|serverlist|…|/` links clicked in a browser,
given on the command line, or pasted with **Paste eD2K Links** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>) in the
server list. See [eD2K links](./ed2k-links.mdx).

njMule can also update the list at each start from addresses of your choice, and grow it from the
servers and clients it meets: see the [Server options](../settings/server.mdx).

### The menu of a server

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Connect To** | Connects to the server; on the server you are on it reads **Disconnect** (<kbd>Enter</kbd>). |
| **Priority** ▸ | **Low**, **Normal** or **High**. |
| **Add To Static List** | Keeps the server in your static list, which is never cleaned of dead servers and can be the only one njMule connects to. |
| **Remove From Static List** | Takes it out of the static list. |
| **Copy eD2K Links** | Copies the server links of the servers chosen (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>). |
| **Paste eD2K Links** | Adds the servers of the server links on the clipboard, and fetches the server lists linked there (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>). |
| **Remove** | Removes the servers chosen (<kbd>Delete</kbd>). |
| **Remove All** | Removes every server, after a question. |

**Remove All** asks **Remove all … servers?**: the list will be empty and njMule will use Kad only,
until you add servers again. **Keep the servers** is the default.

## Kad

The **Kad** part shows how your node stands on Kad, the serverless network.

<Screenshot
    id="kad"
    alt="The Kad part: Kad is on, and other nodes can reach you; the chart Contacts by distance from Near to Far; the facts State Open, UDP state Open, Buddy None, Contacts, Verified contacts and KadID; the closed section Join Kad; the tabs Contacts and Current Searches"
    caption="Kad, with the contacts by distance"
/>

- **A line on top** says the state in plain words and what to do: **Kad is off.** (connect to start
  it), **Kad is looking for the nodes it knows.**, **Kad is on, but other nodes cannot reach you on
  port …** (open the port, or turn on UPnP), or **Kad is on, and other nodes can reach you.**
- **Contacts by distance** draws your contacts as bars, from the nearest to your node's id to the
  farthest; the tooltip of a bar counts its contacts. A routing table with contacts at every
  distance, and many near ones, finds what it looks for in few steps.
- **The facts**: **State** (**Open** or **Firewalled**), **UDP state** (**Open**, **Firewalled**
  or **Unknown** until tested), **Buddy** (**None**, **Connecting**, **Connected**, or **Buddy of a
  firewalled client** when you serve one), **Contacts**, **Verified contacts** and your **KadID**.

Kad starts and stops from its card or the network menu of the top bar. A firewalled node gets a buddy
that relays the clients' callbacks to it; an open one may serve a firewalled one.

### Joining Kad

Kad starts from the nodes it already knows, and from those shipped with njMule. The first time, or
when none answers, give it some. **Join Kad** gathers the ways; it is open while Kad is not connected:

- **Nodes.dat from URL:** type or choose the address of a `nodes.dat` list and press **Download**.
- **Add contacts from nodes.dat...**: adds the contacts of a `nodes.dat` file on your computer.
- **From a client you know:** type a node's address and UDP port, as `host:port`, and press
  **Bootstrap**.

njMule says how many new contacts it took. While Kad is not connected, an eD2K client that runs Kad
is also asked for contacts, so a working eD2K connection brings Kad in too.

### Contacts and current searches

Two tabs list:

- **Contacts**: the contacts of your routing table, nearest first, with their **ID**, **Type**,
  **Distance**, **IP**, after the flag and code of its country when the countries are shown, and **Kad**
  version.
- **Current Searches**: the Kad lookups running now, with their target (**KadID**), their **Type**
  (**Node Lookup**, **Search Keywords**, **Search Sources**, **Notes**, **Store Keyword**, **Store
  File**, **Store Notes**) and the **Answers** received.

They are read again every few seconds while you look at them.

## Log

The **Log** part has three tabs. **Reset** clears the log or the server info.

<Screenshot
    id="log"
    alt="The Log part: the chips All 9, Error 1, Warning 1 and Info 7, a Reset button, the tabs Log, Server Info and My Info, and the log lines with their date and time, a warning and an error in colour with an icon"
    caption="The log, with its chips"
/>

- **Log**: what njMule did, each line after its date and time. Errors, warnings and successes have a
  colour and an icon. The chips **All**, **Error**, **Warning** and **Info** keep one kind, each
  with its count. The last line also shows in the status strip; a click there opens the log.
- **Server Info**: when each server session began (**Connection established on: …**, or
  **Obfuscated connection established on: …**) and what the servers said.
- **My Info**: your **eD2K Network** state, with your address and port, your ID and whether it is a
  High ID or a Low ID; the **eD2K Server** you are on (name, description, address, version, users,
  files, and whether the connection is obfuscated); the **Kad Network** state, with UDP, buddy,
  address and ID; and whether the **Web Interface** is on, with its address.

The text of the log and of the server info can be chosen with the mouse, also across lines, and
copied with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd> or the menu (**Copy**, **Select All**). The log file of
njMule is described on [Logs](../data/logs.mdx).
