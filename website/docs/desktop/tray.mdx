title: Notification area and app menu
description: Keep njMule running in the Windows notification area, with its menu, speed limits and the small MiniMule window, and use the application menu on macOS.
---

On Windows njMule can hide in the **notification area**, next to the clock, instead of the taskbar:
it keeps downloading and uploading while its window is out of the way. On macOS it takes the
**application menu** of the system. On Linux the window minimises as usual.

## The notification area (Windows)

**Minimise to the notification area, next to the clock** is on by default (in
[General options](../settings/general.mdx), under **Startup**; the option shows on Windows only).
While it is on, minimising the window hides it, and the njMule icon appears in the notification area.

With the window hidden:

- **Rest the pointer** on the icon to see the rates, as `njMule - Download: … | Upload: …`.
- **Click** the icon to open [MiniMule](#minimule), a small window with njMule at a glance.
- **Double-click** the icon to bring the main window back.
- **Right-click** the icon to open [its menu](#the-menu-of-the-icon).
- The [messages](./interface.mdx#messages) of njMule, such as a finished download or a new chat
  message, show as notifications of the icon.

When the window comes back, the icon goes. If Windows cannot show the icon, the window comes back by
itself.

### Starting hidden

Turn on **Start minimized** (also under **Startup**, off by default) to start njMule out of sight: in
the notification area while minimising goes there, or minimised on the taskbar otherwise. Together
with **Start njMule when I log in**, njMule then runs in the background from the moment you log in.

### The menu of the icon

A right click on the icon opens njMule's menu:

    caption="The menu of the notification area icon"

- **The head** names njMule with its state and its rates, as `Connected · ↓ 1.26 MB/s · ↑ 95.8 KB/s`.
- **Speed limits**: the **Download** and **Upload** sliders, with **Full speed** and **Slow down**,
  as under the rates of the top bar; see [Speed limits](./interface.mdx#speed-limits).
- **Show njMule** (the default, in bold) brings the window back.
- **Connect** or **Disconnect**: only the one that applies shows. It connects to an eD2K server, or
  leaves it; Kad is started and stopped from the window.
- **Options** brings the window back on the options.
- **Quit njMule** ends njMule, asking first unless you turned the question off; see
  [Closing the window](./interface.mdx#closing-the-window).

The menu closes when you click elsewhere or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

### MiniMule

A click on the icon opens **MiniMule** in the corner of the screen, over the other windows:

    caption="MiniMule"

/>

- **Connected** or **Not Connected**, with a dot.
- **Download** and **Upload**: the rates, with their last half minute drawn between them.
- **Completed**: the downloads finished since njMule started.
- **Free Space**: the room left on the disk of the incoming folder.
- **Open Incoming Folder**, **Options** and **Show njMule**.

It is read again every second while it is open.

## The application menu (macOS)

On macOS the items of the application menu lead into njMule:

- **About njMule** opens njMule's About window.
- **Settings…** opens the options.
- **Quit njMule** asks first, as closing the window does, unless you turned the question off.

The shortcuts of the window use <kbd>Cmd</kbd> on macOS: see
[Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard-shortcuts.mdx).
