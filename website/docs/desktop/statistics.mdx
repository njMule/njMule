**Statistics** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>6</kbd>) shows what njMule moves now, how the rates went over
time, the totals of this session and of all sessions, and, on demand, every statistic it keeps. The
summary under the title tells how long this session has run and what it downloaded and uploaded, as
`This session: 3:42 h · ↓ 2.40 GB · ↑ 611.2 MB`.

## What happens now

The cards at the top show, read every second:

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Download rate** | What you download now, in all. |
| **Upload rate** | What you upload now, in all. |
| **Downloads** | The downloads in the list. |
| **Uploading** | The clients downloading from you now. |
| **On Queue** | The clients waiting in your upload queue. |
| **Shared Files** | The files you share. |

## Transfer rates

The graph draws the download rate (blue) and the upload rate (green) over time; the legend names
them, so the colours are never the only cue. Choose the span with the segment beside the title:

- **10 mins** and **1 h**: one point a second.
- **24 h**: one point a minute, the mean of its seconds.

The scale on the left rounds to whole units of what it shows, and the times of the span are written
under the lines. Rest the pointer on the graph to read the time and the two rates of that moment.
While nothing moved in the span, the graph says **Nothing was downloaded or uploaded in this time.**
Screen readers hear the graph as a sentence with the rates now and the highest.

## Session and cumulative

| Card | Figures |
|---|---|
| **Session** | **Downloaded (this session):**, **Uploaded (this session):**, and **Running (session / total)**: how long this session has run, then all sessions together. |
| **Cumulative** | **Downloaded total:** and **Uploaded total:** of all sessions, and the number of **Sessions**. |

The totals of all sessions are kept in `statistics.properties` in the profile; see
[Files](../data/files.mdx).

## All statistics

**All statistics**, at the bottom, opens a tree of every statistic. Its branches stay open or closed
as you leave them while the values change.

| Branch | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Transfers** | **Session UL:DL Ratio:** and **Cumulative UL:DL Ratio:**, upload to download, as `1 : 2.5`. |
| **Uploads** | For the session: **Uploaded Data**, the **Overhead** (the protocol's bytes besides the file data, over TCP and UDP), **Active Uploads** and **Waiting Uploads**. Cumulative: **Uploaded Data**. |
| **Downloads** | For the session: **Downloaded Data**, the **Overhead**, **Active Downloads (chunks)**, **Found Sources** and **Completed Downloads**. Cumulative: **Downloaded Data**. |
| **Connection** | For the session: **Average Downloadrate**, **Average Uploadrate**, **max. Downloadrate** and **Max Upload Rate**. |
| **Time Statistics** | How long the session has run, and all sessions together. |
| **Clients** | The clients you download from or upload to, by program, with their share. |
| **Servers** | **Working Servers**, **Failed Servers**, **Total**, **Users on Working Servers**, **Files on Working Servers**, **Total Users** and **Total Files**. |
| **Shared Files** | How many, their **Total size**, the **Average file size** and the **Largest Shared File**. |
| **Free Space** | The free space of the disks of the **Temporary Files** and of the **Incoming Files**, with their folders. |

The statistics of each shared file are on [Shared files](./shared-files.mdx#the-files); the speed
limits, in the top bar, are explained on [The main window](./interface.mdx#speed-limits).
