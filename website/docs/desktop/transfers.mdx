**Transfers** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd>) is where your downloads are, and where you see who
downloads from you. The segments at the top choose the list shown, one at a time, each with its
count:

| Segment | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Downloads** | The files you download, and those completed until you clear them. |
| **Uploads** | The clients downloading from you now. |
| **On Queue** | The clients waiting in your upload queue. |
| **Banned** | The addresses banned for a while. |
| **Known Clients** | The clients met in the last 90 days. |

The summary under the title tells how many downloads run and the total download rate, for example
`Downloading: 6 · ↓ 1.26 MB/s`; the same count shows on **Transfers** in the sidebar.

## Adding downloads

- Press **Add links** (top right) and paste eD2K links into the dialog that opens; links already on
  the clipboard are in it. See [eD2K links](./ed2k-links.mdx#the-add-downloads-dialog).
- Download a result of a [search](./search.mdx).
- Paste links in the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>), drag them onto the window, or
  click them in a browser: see [eD2K links](./ed2k-links.mdx).

While the list is empty it offers **Search** and **Paste eD2K Links**, which do the same.

## The list of downloads

### Narrowing the list

Above the list:

- **Chips** keep the downloads in one state, each with how many are in it: **All**, **Downloading**,
  **Waiting**, **No sources**, **Paused** (stopped ones too), **Erroneous** (those short of disk
  space too) and **Complete**.
- **All categories** shows the downloads of every category; choose one to see only its downloads.
  The button beside it opens the **Categories** dialog: see [Categories](./categories.mdx).
- **Filter the downloads** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) keeps the downloads whose name holds every
  word you type, whatever the case. <kbd>Esc</kbd> empties it.

### Columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **File Name** | The name, after an icon of what the file holds and the dot of its category (the tooltip names the category). On Windows the icon is the one Windows gives the kind of file, that of the program that opens it. |
| **Size** | The size of the file. |
| **Progress** | A thin bar of what is downloaded, in the colour of the state, with the percentage beside it. |
| **Speed** | The rate it downloads at now, or `—`. |
| **Sources** | The sources sending now out of those known, as `6/48`; `—` once complete. |
| **Status** | The state, on a chip with its own icon. |
| **Remaining** | The time left at the current speed, then how much is missing, as `9:34 mins (102.1 MB)`; `—` for the time while nothing arrives. |
| **Completed** | How much is downloaded (hidden at first). |
| **Priority** | The download priority; an automatic one shows what it acts as, as **Auto [Hi]** (hidden at first). |
| **Category** | The category's name (hidden at first). |

The **⋮** button at the right end of the column heads opens the column menu, which shows or hides
each column. Every list of njMule remembers its columns: their order, widths, which ones show, and
the sort.

### States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Downloading** | At least one source is sending data now. |
| **Waiting** | Sources are known, but none sends yet: you are in their queues. |
| **No sources** | No source has the file right now; njMule keeps asking the servers and Kad. |
| **Paused** | Paused by you; the sources are kept. |
| **Stopped** | Stopped by you: paused, with its sources forgotten. |
| **Insufficient disk space** | The disk of the temporary files is full; the download goes on by itself once there is room again. |
| **Erroneous** | The file could not be written; **Resume** tries again. |
| **Complete** | Finished, its file in the incoming folder. |

### The progress bar

The thin bar in **Progress** takes the colour of the state, and the percentage is always written
beside it:

| Colour | State |
|---|---|
| Blue | **Downloading** |
| Grey | **Waiting**, **Paused**, **Stopped** |
| Orange | **No sources** |
| Red | **Erroneous**, **Insufficient disk space** |
| Green | **Complete** |

The bar of each part, with what every colour means, is in the [details](#the-details-pane).

### Sources under a download

A download opens onto its sources, a line each under it, in the columns of the list:

- **Double-click** it, or choose it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to open or close it.
- Press <kbd>→</kbd> to open the chosen downloads, <kbd>←</kbd> to close them.

Each source line shows a dot of what it does and its name (or address, in the tooltip too), what it
sent under **Size**, the share of the file it has under **Progress**, its speed under **Speed**, its
program and its place in its queue (`QR: 33`) under **Sources**, and what it does under **Status**.
The sources sending come first, then the others by their place in the queue.

| Dot | Status of the source |
|---|---|
| Green | **Transferring**: it sends you data. |
| Yellow | **On Queue**: you wait in its queue. |
| Red | **Connecting** or **Asking**: njMule is reaching it. |
| Dark grey | **Queue Full**, **No needed parts** or **Asked for another file**: of no use for now. |
| `?` in a ring | **Waiting**: not asked yet. |

A source **Asked for another file** has the file of several of your downloads and is asked for one
at a time; see [sources shared with other files](#sources-shared-with-other-files).

### Completed downloads

A completed download stays in the list, also after a restart, until you take it off:

- **Double-click** it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to open its file in the program your system uses
  for that kind of file.
- **Open Folder** in its menu opens its folder in your file manager, with the file selected.
- **Clear Completed** (top right, or at the bottom of the menu) takes every completed download off
  the list. <kbd>Delete</kbd> takes off the completed downloads chosen. Their files always stay where
  they are.

If the file was moved or deleted since, njMule says **Requested file not found**.

## The details pane

**Details** (top right, or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>) shows the details of the download
chosen under the list. Drag the divider to give them more or less room: njMule remembers the height,
and keeps it tall enough for the buttons. The cross at the end of the tabs, or **Details** again,
hides them.

On the left is the download itself:

- Its icon and name, with its size, category and priority.
- The share done in large figures, how much of how much, and the speed with the time left (or the
  state while it does not download).
- **The part map**, the whole file drawn from start to end, with its [legend](#the-part-map).
- A note when you need to act: **No source has this file right now: njMule keeps asking the servers
  and Kad.**, **The disk is full: free some space and the download goes on by itself.**, or **The file could not be
  written: resume to try again.**
- Two buttons for what the state asks: **Pause** and **Stop** while it downloads or waits, **Pause**
  and **Add Sources...** with no source, **Resume** and **Stop** while paused, **Resume** once
  stopped, **Resume** and **Open Incoming Folder** after an error, **Pause** and **Open Incoming
  Folder** with a full disk, **Open Incoming Folder** once complete.
- The copy button (**Copy eD2K link**, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>) and **⋯** with **Preview**,
  **Comments...**, **Priority**, **Category**, **Add Sources...** and **Cancel download**.

With several downloads chosen, the pane says **Downloads chosen: …** and offers **Pause**,
**Resume**, **Stop**, **Cancel downloads** and **Copy eD2K link** for all of them; with none, it asks
you to choose one.

### The part map

The part map shows where the file stands, part by part. The words of the legend are always under it:

| Colour (light theme) | Legend | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Dark grey | **Downloaded** | This piece is on your disk. |
| Yellow | **Being requested** | A source is being asked for this piece now. |
| Pale to strong blue | **Missing, bluer the more sources have it** | Missing, but sources have it: the more sources (up to ten), the stronger the blue. |
| Soft rose | **Missing, no source has it** | Missing, and no source known has it. |
| Green line on top | **Share completed (top line)** | How much of the file is done, as a thin line along the top. |

In the dark theme the downloaded pieces are light and the blues brighter; under Windows high
contrast the map takes the system's colours. A download that has just started is all rose until its
sources tell what they have.

### Tabs

- **Sources** (with their count): each source with its name, country and program, what it does with
  its dot, how much it sent or its place in the queue (`Queue rank 33`), and a bar of the share of
  the file it has. Its menu has **View Shared Files**, which asks the client for the files it shares
  (it may refuse), and **Ask this source for this file** for a source asked for another file. While
  there is no source, **Add Sources...** is there too.
- **Details**: **Status**, **Size**, **Completed**, **Parts**, **Datarate**, **Sources**,
  **Transferring**, **Priority**, **Category** and **Remaining**, then the file's **Hash** with a
  **Copy** button.
- **Source Names**: the names the sources give the file, with how many sources give each, the most
  given first. See [renaming a download](#renaming-a-download).
- **Comments**: the comments and ratings the sources give the file, with **Comments...** to write
  your own. See [comments and ratings](#comments-and-ratings).

### Renaming a download

The name a download gets once complete can be changed while it runs:

1. Open the **Source Names** tab.
2. Choose a name and press **Take over** (or double-click it): it goes into the box under the list.
   You can also type a name there.
3. Press **Cleanup** to tidy it: escapes, dots and underscores used as spaces and tags in square
   brackets go, and each word starts with a capital (the words to drop are an option of
   [Directories](../settings/directories.mdx)).
4. Press **Rename**. The name is made safe for any file system.

## The menu of a download

Right-click the downloads chosen. The commands that do not apply are greyed out, never hidden.

    caption="The menu of the downloads"

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pause** | Pauses the download; its sources are kept. |
| **Resume** | Resumes a download paused, stopped or in error. |
| **Stop** | Pauses the download and forgets its sources. |
| **Cancel download** | Cancels the download after a question, deleting what was downloaded; see [Cancelling](#cancelling-and-undo). |
| **Details** | Shows the [details pane](#the-details-pane). |
| **Preview** | Opens what is downloaded so far in your video player; see [Preview](#preview). |
| **Open Folder** | For a completed download: opens its folder, the file selected. |
| **Comments...** | Your comment and rating of the file, and those of others; see [Comments](#comments-and-ratings). |
| **Priority** ▸ | **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Auto**. Higher priorities ask for sources and start transfers first; **Auto** gives rare files a higher one. |
| **Category** ▸ | **(Unassign)** or one of your categories; see [Categories](./categories.mdx). |
| **More** ▸ **Add Sources...** | Adds a source you know by its address; see [Adding a source](#adding-a-source). |
| **More** ▸ **Try to download preview chunks first** | Downloads the first and the last parts first, so that a film can be previewed sooner. |
| **More** ▸ **Sources shared with other files** ▸ | Moves the sources this download shares with others; see [below](#sources-shared-with-other-files). |
| **Copy eD2K link** | Copies the links of the downloads chosen, one per line (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>). |
| **Copy eD2K Links (HTML)** | Copies them as HTML links for a web page. |
| **Clear Completed** | Takes every completed download off the list, their files kept. |

### Cancelling and undo

Cancelling deletes what was downloaded, so njMule asks first, naming the files (up to five):
**Cancel this download?** or **Cancel … downloads?**, with **Cancel download** in red and **Keep
downloading** as the default. <kbd>Delete</kbd> asks the same for the downloads chosen.

Once cancelled, the message **Downloads cancelled: …** offers **Undo** for ten seconds: it brings the
downloads back with what they had. Shortly after, their files are deleted for good.

### Adding a source

**Add Sources...** asks for the **Address of a user who has the file**: a host or an IP address, a
colon, then the TCP port, such as `192.0.2.10:4662` (an IPv6 address goes in brackets, as
`[2001:db8::1]:4662`). njMule says **Source … added**, or **Source … not added: known already or
refused by the IP filter**. A source at a LAN address can be added this way too.

### Preview

**Preview** starts the video player chosen in the options with what is downloaded so far. Choose the
player, and its arguments if needed, under **Video Player** in [Directories](../settings/directories.mdx);
without one njMule says **Choose a video player in the settings first**. Until the start of the
file has arrived, it says **The start of the file has not arrived yet: try the preview again later**.
**Try to download preview chunks first** makes a preview possible sooner; the option of the same name
in Directories applies it to every download that can be previewed.

### Comments and ratings

**Comments...** opens **Rate and comment**:

1. Choose a **File Quality**: **Not rated**, **Invalid / Corrupt / Fake**, **Poor**, **Fair**,
   **Good** or **Excellent**.
2. Type a **File comment:**, one line of at most 128 characters. The clients downloading the file
   from you see it.
3. Press **Save**.

Under **Comments** are those of the sources of the download, and the notes Kad holds on the file,
marked with the Kad icon; the name the commenter gave the file is in the tooltip. **Search Kad** looks
for more notes on Kad. Comments holding a filtered word are left out.

### Sources shared with other files

A client that has the files of several of your downloads is asked for one at a time; the others
list it as **Asked for another file**. njMule moves such sources by itself to where they are most
useful, and you can move them by hand under **More** ▸ **Sources shared with other files**:

- **Take the sources shared with other files now**: their sources are asked for this download (for
  downloads that run).
- **Take sources shared with other files whenever possible**: this download takes them by itself
  from now on (a tick, kept with the download).
- **Give this file's shared sources to the other files**.

njMule says how many sources moved (**Sources swapped: …**). One source can also be moved from the
**Sources** tab of the details, with **Ask this source for this file**; a source being asked or
sending data cannot move then.

## Uploads, queue, bans and clients

### Uploads and On Queue

**Uploads** lists the clients downloading from you, **On Queue** those waiting for a slot, with the
same columns: **User Name**, **Country**, **Software**, **File Name**, **Speed**, **Transferred** and
**Waited**. Their menu has **View Shared Files**, which asks the client for its list of files, shown
in a window where you can download them.

### Banned

**Banned** lists the addresses banned for a while, for misbehaving (such as asking too often or
sending corrupt data), with the **Time left**. **Unban** in their menu lifts the ban.

### Known Clients

**Known Clients** lists the clients met in the last 90 days: **User Name**, **Country**,
**Software**, **First seen**, **Last Seen**, and the bytes exchanged with each of all times
(**Uploaded total**, **Downloaded total**). It keeps the name and program each gave last and, when
countries are shown, where it was from, never its address.

### Countries and flags

When **Show the country of other clients** is on (in [General options](../settings/general.mdx), off
by default), the sources, the uploads, the queue and the known clients show the country of each
client: its flag beside its code, and its name in the tooltip. njMule then downloads DB-IP's free
country database once a month.

## Downloads that receive nothing

njMule can delete by itself the downloads that receive nothing for a number of days. It is off by
default; see **Delete downloads that receive nothing for (days, 0 = never)** in
[Directories](../settings/directories.mdx). Paused and stopped downloads are never deleted, and the
link of each one deleted is first written to `deleted-downloads.txt` in the profile folder, so that
you can add it again.
