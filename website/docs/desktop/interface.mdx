The njMule window has the same frame on every screen: a **sidebar** of places on the left, a **top
bar** with the screen's title, a field to type into, the rates and the state of the networks, the
**screen** itself in the middle, and a **status strip** at the bottom. This page explains each part,
so that you know where to look and what every element does.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the places of njMule in three groups. Each one also opens with a shortcut, in the
order of the sidebar (on macOS use <kbd>Cmd</kbd> instead of <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>):

| Place | Shortcut | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Transfers** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> | Downloads, uploads, the upload queue, banned and known clients: see [Transfers](./transfers.mdx) |
| **Search** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>2</kbd> | Searches on eD2K and Kad, one tab each: see [Search](./search.mdx) |
| **Shared Files** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>3</kbd> | The folders and files you share: see [Shared files](./shared-files.mdx) |
| **Messages** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>4</kbd> | Chats and friends: see [Messages](./messages.mdx) |
| **Network** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>5</kbd> | eD2K, Kad and your ports, the servers, Kad and the log: see [Network](./network.mdx) |
| **Statistics** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>6</kbd> | Rates over time and every figure: see [Statistics](./statistics.mdx) |
| **Options** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>7</kbd> | All the settings: see [Options](../settings/index.mdx) |

The first four are what you do every day; **Network** and **Statistics** come after a line;
**Options** stays at the bottom. A place tells what waits there:

- **Transfers** shows the number of downloads running (up to `99+`).
- **Messages** shows the number of messages that arrived while you were on another screen; opening
  **Messages** clears it.
- **Network** shows a dot: in the "connected" colour while eD2K or Kad is up, in the "connecting"
  colour while njMule is trying, and empty while both are off. Its tooltip names the state of both
  networks.

The tooltip of every place repeats its name and its shortcut. njMule opens on the screen you left
last time.

### Folding the sidebar

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>, or the button at the top right of the sidebar (**Collapse the
sidebar**), to fold the sidebar to its icons. Folded, the button under the logo (**Expand the
sidebar**) opens it again, and the counts become small marks on the corner of the icons. njMule
remembers your choice.

In a window narrower than about 1000 pixels the sidebar folds by itself, and opens again as you left
it once the window is wider. In such a narrow window the other parts keep to their essentials too:
the tool buttons show only their icons, the summary under the title is hidden, the command field
shows only its shortcut, and the rates show only the download rate.

### Buy me a coffee

**Buy me a coffee**, just above **Options**, opens the page where you can support the development of
njMule, in your system's browser. It never changes anything in njMule.

## The top bar

From left to right the top bar holds:

1. **The title** of the screen, and under it a **summary** of what it holds, for example
   `Downloading: 6 · ↓ 1.26 MB/s` on Transfers, `Searches: 2 · running: 0` on Search or
   `eD2K: Lighthouse (HighID) · Kad: Open` on Network. Options says what applies at once.
2. **The command field**, which opens the [command palette](#the-command-palette).
3. **The rates**: the download and upload rates, with their last half minute drawn beside them as
   two small lines. Click them to open the [speed limits](#speed-limits).
4. **The network control**, which tells whether you are connected and opens the
   [network menu](#the-network-menu).
5. **⋯** (**More**), the [menu of tools](#the-more-menu).

### The command palette

The palette lets you do almost anything by typing. Open it with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, by
clicking the field **Type a command, a search or an eD2K link** in the top bar, or simply by
typing a letter while that field has the focus.

<Screenshot
    id="palette"

    caption="The command palette (Ctrl+K)"

What you type finds:

- **The places** of the sidebar and **the commands** of the menus: connect or disconnect, **Open
  Incoming Folder**, **Set up njMule**, **Import from eMule...**, **Light or dark**, **Collapse the
  sidebar** or **Expand the sidebar**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Check for new version** and **About
  njMule**. A command is listed when its name holds every word you typed.
- **A search of the network**: the last line, **Search the network for “…”**, opens Search and
  starts a search for the words typed, as the search box would.
- **Downloads**: when you paste eD2K file links, the line **Download: …** names the files and adds
  them as downloads at once.

Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to choose a line, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to run it, and <kbd>Esc</kbd>
or a click outside the palette to close it.

### Speed limits

A click on the rates opens **Speed limits**, the same sliders as in the menu of the
[notification area](./tray.mdx):

- **Download** and **Upload** each have a slider, from **No limit** on the far left up to the
  capacity of your line on the far right, on a logarithmic scale so that both a few KB/s and many
  MB/s are easy to reach. The limit applies as soon as you let the slider go, and is written beside
  it.
- **Full speed** sets both limits to the capacity of your line.
- **Slow down** sets both limits to the lowest value, `1.0 KB/s`.

The capacity of your line and the limits are also options: see [Bandwidth](../settings/bandwidth.mdx).

### The network menu

The network control shows a dot, the overall state (**Connected**, **Connecting** or **Not
Connected**) and, under it, a short detail such as `High ID · Kad: Open`. Its tooltip names the
state of both networks. Click it to open its menu:

- **eD2K**: the server you are on with `(HighID)` or `(LowID)`, its users and files, and a button
  that reads **Connect**, **Cancel** (while connecting) or **Disconnect**.
- **Kad**: its state (**Off**, **Not Connected**, **Open** or **Firewalled**, with `UDP open` or
  `UDP firewalled` once Kad has tested it), the number of contacts, and **Connect** or
  **Disconnect**.
- **Choose another server…** opens the server list.
- **Network** opens the Network place (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>5</kbd>).
- The wide button at the bottom acts on both networks: **Connect** connects to an eD2K server and
  starts Kad (when Kad is on in the options), **Cancel** stops trying servers, **Disconnect** leaves
  both. It stands out in the accent colour only while you are disconnected.

A click on the eD2K or the Kad line opens the network at the servers or at Kad. To learn what the
states mean, see [Servers and Kad](../network/servers-and-kad.mdx) and [Ports](../network/ports.mdx).

### The More menu

**⋯** (**More**) holds what the window does besides its places:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open Incoming Folder** | Opens the folder of finished downloads in your file manager. |
| **Options** | Opens the options (as <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>7</kbd>). |
| **Set up njMule** | Opens the first-start wizard again, filled in with your current settings: see [First run](../getting-started/first-run.mdx). |
| **Import from eMule...** | Opens the import of an eMule profile: see [Import from eMule](../getting-started/import-emule.mdx). |
| **Connect to a remote njMule** | Shows and drives the njMule of another computer: see [Remote njMule](./remote.mdx). |
| **Light or dark** | Switches between the light and the dark version of the theme: see [Appearance](./appearance.mdx). |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | Lists the shortcuts in a small window (<kbd>F1</kbd>): see [Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard-shortcuts.mdx). |
| **Check for new version** | Looks for a new version now: see [Updates](../updates.mdx). |
| **About njMule** | The version, the copyright and the licence, with **Copy version details** to paste into a problem report. |

## The banner

A line above the screen tells of a state that lasts and needs you to do something. It shows two
seconds after the state begins, so that a quick reconnection does not make it flicker:

| Banner | Button |
|---|---|
| **Not connected: searches and downloads wait for a network.** Neither an eD2K server nor Kad is connected or being tried. | **Connect** connects both networks. |
| **Others cannot reach you on port … (LowID): open it in your router, or turn on UPnP in the options.** You have a LowID on eD2K or Kad is firewalled, and no network reaches you. | **Options** opens the options. |
| **The network interface … is not available: njMule stays offline until it is back.** njMule is bound to a network interface (such as a VPN) that is gone. | **Options** opens the options. |

The cross closes the banner; it comes back only when the state changes. For the ports and the
network interface, see [Ports](../network/ports.mdx) and [Network interface](../network/bind-interface.mdx).

## Messages

Short messages appear at the bottom of the window, above the status strip, at most two at a time: a
new one pushes out the oldest. They come in three kinds:

- **News**, such as **New message from …** or **Download added: …**, which go after six seconds.
- **Successes**, such as **Download finished: …**, which go after six seconds too.
- **Problems**, such as a connection refused, which stay until you close them with their cross.

The same message again does not stack: the one shown counts up (`×2`). Some messages carry a
button and stay ten seconds, so that you have time to press it: **Undo** after cancelling
downloads, **Details** when a new version is out, **Restart now** when it is ready to install.

When the window is hidden in the notification area of Windows, the messages also show there: see
[Notification area](./tray.mdx). A finished download and a new chat message can also play a sound:
see [General options](../settings/general.mdx).

## Questions and boxes

When njMule asks something, the title of the question says what is decided, and the buttons say
what they do, such as **Cancel download** and **Keep downloading**, in the order your system puts
them. The default answer is ringed, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> chooses it. In a question about something
that cannot be undone, such as cancelling downloads or removing every server, the default is the safe
answer and the risky one is outlined in red. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

Every box takes only its kind of value: a number within its limits, a time, an address, a folder,
eD2K links or a search. Characters that cannot belong there are dropped as you type or paste them; a
box whose text is not valid yet is marked, says in its tooltip what it takes, and keeps its button
off until it is right.

Sizes and rates are written in the units of your language; a rate switches to MB/s from a thousand
KB/s on, and to GB/s from a thousand MB/s.

## The status strip

The strip at the bottom of the window shows, from left to right:

- **The last event** of the log, such as `Download completed: …`. Click it to open the log.
- **The update**, while a new version downloads (`Downloading njMule …: …%`) or once it is ready
  (`njMule … is ready: restart to update`). Click it to open the Updates window.
- **The users and files** of the networks: `Users … | Files …` adds up every server of your list
  that answers while you are connected to eD2K; with Kad up too, Kad's estimate of its network
  follows each figure in brackets. With Kad alone, the figures are Kad's estimate.
- **The limits**, such as `Limits: ↓ No limit · ↑ 250.0 KB/s`. Click them to open the
  [speed limits](#speed-limits) over the strip.
- **The free space** of the disk where finished downloads go (`Free Space: …`), read every ten
  seconds; the tooltip names the folder.

## Adding downloads by dragging

Drag eD2K file links from a web page or a text onto any part of the window: when the text starts
with an `ed2k://|file|` link, every file link in it becomes a download, and a message names each one
added. The other ways to add links are on [eD2K links](./ed2k-links.mdx).

## Closing the window

Closing the window ends njMule, and with it the downloads and uploads. By default njMule asks
first: **Quit njMule?** with **Quit njMule** and **Keep running**. Tick **Do not ask me again** to
stop the question, or turn **Prompt on exit** on or off in [General options](../settings/general.mdx).
On Windows you can instead minimise njMule into the [notification area](./tray.mdx), where it keeps
running.

njMule remembers the size and place of the window, and whether it was maximised. It keeps the window
large enough for every button to show; a window of 900×600 still shows them all.
