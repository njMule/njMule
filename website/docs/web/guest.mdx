---
title: Guest access
description: Give the web interface a second, read-only password for people who may look at your transfers, searches, servers, Kad and statistics but change nothing.
---

A guest is someone who signs in to the web interface with a password of its own and may only look:
your family checking whether a download is done, or you on a borrowed computer where you would
rather not type your real password. njMule has no guest until you give it a password; there is at
most one guest password, shared by everyone you give it to.

## Set the guest password

The guest password needs at least 8 characters and must differ from yours.

In the desktop app on the computer that runs njMule:

1. Open **Options → Web Interface**.
2. Type the password twice in **Guest password, twice (a guest may only look)**.
3. The web interface takes the guest password at once.

**No guest** takes the guest away, again at once. In a browser, even signed in with your own
password, the page shows these settings greyed out: *Only the desktop app on njMule's computer
changes these settings.*

### On a headless njMule

A njMule without the desktop app (on a server, in Docker, on a Synology NAS, as the Windows
service) takes the guest password from the first line of a file, as it takes yours:
`--webui-guest-password-file <file>`. `--webui-no-guest` takes the guest away. Both come with
`--webui-password`, `--webui-password-file` or `--service=install`:

```bash
njmule --webui-password --webui-guest-password-file ~/njmule-guest-password.txt
njmule --webui-password-file /run/secrets/njmule_webui_password \
    --webui-guest-password-file /run/secrets/njmule_webui_guest_password
njmule --service=install --webui-guest-password-file C:\Users\you\njmule-guest-password.txt
```

With `--webui-password-file` alone, the guest's file is read again at every start, as yours is.
Without either option the guest stays as it was, also when you set your own password again.

- **Docker:** a second secret, see [Install with Docker](../getting-started/install-docker.mdx).
- **Synology:** the installation wizard asks for a guest password too; leave it empty for no guest.

## What a guest sees

The guest signs in on the same page as you, with the guest password. The top bar then says
**Guest** (its tooltip tells what a guest sees), and the sidebar, the shortcuts and the command
palette offer only the places a guest may open.

<Screenshot id="web-guest" alt="The Transfers place as a guest sees it: only Transfers, Search, Network and Statistics in the sidebar, the word Guest in the top bar beside the rates, and no Add links button." caption="Transfers, as a guest sees them." />

| Place | What the guest sees | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| **Transfers** | The downloads with their details, sources, names and comments; the uploads and the queue | Any action on a download; the banned and the known clients |
| **Search** | The searches made through the web interface, with their results | Start, close or download a search |
| **Network** | The eD2K, Kad and port cards; the servers; Kad's state, contacts and searches | Connect, add or change servers, join Kad, test the ports; the log |
| **Statistics** | The rates and figures | |

A guest never sees **Shared Files**, **Messages** or **Options**, nor the speed limits, the network
control of the top bar or the log line of the status strip. **Copy eD2K link** and the filters
still work, since they change nothing; the other items of the right-click menus are greyed out. Of
the live notices, a guest gets only those of finished downloads. A guest ends its session as you
do, with **⋯ → Logout**.

The searches a guest sees are those made in a browser through the web interface, or by the
[remote-control API](../server/remote-control.mdx) on its port; the desktop app's own searches are
not among them.

## What protects it

The page a guest gets leaves out what it may not do, but that is only for comfort: njMule itself
refuses every change a guest's session asks for, and every read outside the list above (the
settings, the log, the chats, the shared files and the banned and known clients), answering
*A guest can only look at the transfers, searches, servers, Kad and statistics.* A guest's session
otherwise follows the same rules as yours: the same [lockout](./security.mdx#failed-logins) after
wrong passwords, the same idle time of 30 minutes. njMule logs each guest login with its address.

:::note
A guest sees the names of the files you download and the searches made through the web
interface. Give the password only to people who may know them.
:::
